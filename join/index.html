      Join the lab

      Join the lab

      We are interested in excellent students and postdocs who want to work on AI-driven protein science at the interface of bioinformatics, structural biology, and evolution.

      Areas of interest

      • protein interactions and complexes
      • proteoforms and proteomics-aware modelling
      • domain evolution and structural diversification
      • membrane proteins and topology
      • deep learning for molecular systems

      Who should apply?

      We welcome applicants with backgrounds in:

      • bioinformatics
      • computer science
      • machine learning
      • structural biology
      • physics
      • mathematics
      • statistics
      • computational chemistry
      • related quantitative disciplines

      Current openings

      • Two PhD students: We are currently recruiting two PhD students. See the official announcement and application details here: https://su.varbi.com/en/what:job/jobID:912651/

      Master’s thesis students

      We also accept strong master’s students for thesis projects, for students enrolled at a Swedish university.

      Why join this environment?

      The lab combines methodological development with biologically important questions and is connected to Stockholm University and SciLifeLab. Current work spans protein complexes, membrane proteins, proteoforms, and protein evolution, with access to strong collaborations and national computational infrastructure.

      What to send

      If you are interested in joining the group, please send:

      • a short statement of interest
      • your CV
      • links to publications, code, or thesis work
      • names of referees or references

      Finding accommodation

      Finding housing in the Stockholm area can be challenging, so start looking early. First-hand rental contracts have long queues, but there is a large second-hand rental market with several websites dedicated to it.

      Stockholm University resources

      • Find housing in Stockholm: tips, links, and advice on how to avoid rental fraud
      • Accommodation for researchers and doctoral students: the university’s own accommodation options

      Second-hand rentals (external sites)

      • Qasa: large second-hand marketplace
      • Samtrygg: large second-hand marketplace
      • Kvalster: overview of listings from many sites
      • Wåhlin Fastigheter: apartments allocated by lottery

      Housing queues

      Many housing companies allocate apartments to whoever has been in their queue the longest. If you plan to stay in Sweden for a longer time, it is worth signing up early, even if you cannot get an apartment right away.

      • Bostadsförmedlingen i Stockholm: the official Stockholm city housing queue
      • Heimstaden: allocation strictly by queue time
      • HomeQ: many housing companies, most (but not all) queue-based
      • Wenner-Gren Foundations: guest researcher accommodation (postdocs only)
